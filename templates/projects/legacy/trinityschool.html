Trinity International School

CEC completed the construction and renovation of a G+2 complex for Trinity International Academy in Nyarutarama, including full Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) works, with classrooms, a science lab, multipurpose halls, and sports facilities.

LOCATION
Nyarutarama, Kigali
PROJECT TYPE
Educational Facility
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

Civil & Structural Works

Execution of the substructure and superstructure, including structural concrete works and masonry.

02

Electrical Systems

Installation of electrical infrastructure including power distribution, lighting, and backup systems.

03

Plumbing Systems

Implementation of plumbing and sanitary systems throughout the school complex.

04

Mechanical Works (HVAC)

Installation of air conditioning and ventilation systems for classrooms and other facilities.

05

Interior Finishing

Completion of internal finishes including ceilings, partitions, flooring, doors, and painting.

06

Sports & Outdoor Facilities

Development of sports facilities and external works to support student activities.
