Kilinda Substation Upgrade

CEC carried out the EPC upgrade of the Kilinda Substation transformer capacity from 1.6 MVA to 10 MVA, strengthening the substation's capacity and improving power-system reliability.

LOCATION
Kilinda Substation, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
Substation Upgrade / EPC
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Energy Development Corporation Limited (EDCL)

SCOPE OF WORK

01

Plant Design & Detailed Engineering

Plant design and detailed engineering for the upgrade of the Kilinda transformer from 1.6 MVA to 10 MVA.

02

Procurement & Supply

Procurement and supply of the 10 MVA power transformer and equipment required for the upgrade.

03

Transformer Erection & Installation

Erection and installation of the 10 MVA power transformer.

04

MV Protection & Control Systems

Upgrade of Medium Voltage protection and control systems.

05

LV Protection & Control Systems

Upgrade of Low Voltage protection and control systems.

06

Associated Cabling

Associated cabling works for the transformer and MV/LV protection and control systems.

07

Civil Works

Execution of civil works associated with the Kilinda transformer upgrade.

08

Commissioning

Commissioning of the upgraded transformer and associated systems.
