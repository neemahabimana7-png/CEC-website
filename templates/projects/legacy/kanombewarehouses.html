Kanombe Warehouses

CEC delivered the Kanombe Warehouses project through a combination of earthworks, civil works and pre-engineered steel structures.

The development also incorporated fire suppression, electrical and CCTV surveillance systems, together with heavy-duty asphalt parking areas and a covered hangar.

LOCATION
Kanombe, Kigali
PROJECT TYPE
Industrial & Warehouse Development
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

EARTHWORKS & CIVIL WORKS

Execution of earthworks and civil construction required for the development of the warehouse facilities.

02

PRE-ENGINEERED STEEL STRUCTURES

Delivery and erection of pre-engineered steel structures forming the principal warehouse buildings.

03

FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEM

Installation of the fire suppression system serving the warehouse development.

04

ELECTRICAL INSTALLATION

Delivery of electrical installations required to support the operation of the warehouse facilities.

05

CCTV SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM

Installation of CCTV surveillance infrastructure to support security and monitoring across the development.

06

PARKING & COVERED HANGAR

Development of heavy-duty asphalt parking areas together with the covered hangar included in the project.
