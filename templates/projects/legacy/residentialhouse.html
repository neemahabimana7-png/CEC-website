Residential House – Kimihurura

In Kimihurura, Century Engineering Contractors constructed a G+1 residential building comprising a main double-storey house, an outbuilding, a swimming pool, a relaxation shed, a guard house, and associated external works.

The project also incorporated complete Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) installations to support the functionality and comfort of the property.

LOCATION
Kimihurura, Kigali
PROJECT TYPE
Residential Development
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

MAIN RESIDENCE

Construction of the main G+1 residential building forming the principal double-storey house of the development.

02

OUTBUILDING & SUPPORT FACILITIES

Delivery of the outbuilding, relaxation shed and guard house forming the supporting facilities of the property.

03

SWIMMING POOL

Construction of the swimming pool as part of the residential development and its external amenities.

04

MECHANICAL INSTALLATIONS

Integration of mechanical installations required to support the functionality and comfort of the residential property.

05

ELECTRICAL & PLUMBING INSTALLATIONS

Delivery of electrical and plumbing installations as part of the complete MEP works for the property.

06

EXTERNAL WORKS

Execution of the associated external works surrounding and supporting the residential development.
