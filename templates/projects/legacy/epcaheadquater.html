EPCA Group
Headquarters

Century Engineering Contractors (CEC) was responsible for the construction of the two-storey building that now serves as the headquarters of EPC Africa Group.

The project included the design and execution of the building's substructure and superstructure, together with comprehensive Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) works to support the building's functionality.
