Nyabarongo
Substation

CEC carried out the EPC upgrade of Nyabarongo Hydropower Plant Substation to 110/30kV, 20MVA, including new feeder construction and load transfer from Gatumba and Ngororero to improve power reliability.

LOCATION
Nyabarongo, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
110/30kV 20MVA Substation Upgrade / EPC
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Energy Development Corporation Limited (EDCL)

SCOPE OF WORK

01

Plant Design & Engineering

Plant design for the upgrade of Nyabarongo Hydropower Plant substation to 110/30kV, 20MVA.

02

Procurement & Supply

Supply of plant and equipment for the EPC substation upgrade.

03

Erection Works

Erection of plant and equipment for the upgraded 110/30kV, 20MVA substation.

04

New Feeder Construction

Construction of a new feeder from Nyabarongo I Substation.

05

Load Transfer Works

Transfer of loads from the Gatumba and Ngororero feeders to the new feeders from Nyabarongo I.

06

Commissioning

Commissioning of the upgraded Nyabarongo substation.
