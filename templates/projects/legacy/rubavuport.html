Port of Rubavu

Century Engineering Contractors (CEC) carried out the Design, Procurement and Construction works for the development of the Port of Rubavu.

The project included offshore/in-water quay construction together with port infrastructure required to support port operations.

LOCATION
Rubavu, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
Port / Marine Infrastructure
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Rwanda Transport Development Agency (RTDA)
SCOPE OF WORK
01

Design & Procurement

Engineering design and procurement activities supporting the port development.

02

Quay Construction

Offshore and in-water construction of the main quay structure.

03

Cargo Storage

Construction of cargo-storage infrastructure supporting port operations.

04

Passenger Terminal

Construction of passenger-terminal facilities for the port.

05

Marine & Utility Infrastructure

Delivery of the police pontoon, floating-boat facilities, port utilities and power substation.

06

Roads Network

Construction of the supporting road network serving the port.
