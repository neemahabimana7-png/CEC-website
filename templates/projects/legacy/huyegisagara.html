Huye–Gisagara & Gasarenda–Kitabi Street Lighting Projects

CEC carried out street lighting works along Huye–Gisagara Road and Gasarenda–Kitabi Road under framework contracts for the construction of MV and LV electrical lines, service connections, and associated works, including associated civil works for the Gasarenda–Kitabi project.

LOCATION
Huye–Gisagara & Gasarenda–Kitabi, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
Street Lighting / MV & LV Electrical Lines
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
EDCL — Energy Development Corporation Limited

SCOPE OF WORK

01

Line Surveying

Line surveying for the Huye–Gisagara and Gasarenda–Kitabi projects.

02

Excavation for Pole Foundations

Excavation for pole foundations along both project routes.

03

Erection of Poles

Erection of poles for the electrical lines and street lighting works.

04

Stringing Works

Stringing works for the electrical lines on both projects.

05

Installation of Lamps

Installation of 278 LED streetlights along Huye–Gisagara Road and 283 LED streetlights along the 9.9 km Gasarenda–Kitabi project.

06

Transformers (Huye–Gisagara)

Four transformers included in the Huye–Gisagara project.

07

Medium Voltage (MV) Line — Huye–Gisagara

1.73 km of Medium Voltage electrical line.

08

Low Voltage (LV) Lines — Huye–Gisagara

9.76 km of Low Voltage electrical lines.

09

Security Cameras — Gasarenda–Kitabi

Installation of security cameras as part of the associated works.

10

Commissioning

Commissioning works for the electrical lines and street lighting installations on both projects.
