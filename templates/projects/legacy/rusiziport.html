Rusizi Port

Rusizi Port is a major marine infrastructure development being delivered through the design, procurement and construction of the port and its supporting facilities.

The development combines an offshore jetty with cargo, passenger, utility, power and road infrastructure to create the facilities required for efficient port operations.

LOCATION
Rusizi, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
Port & Marine Infrastructure
STATUS
Under Construction
CLIENT
Rwanda Transport Development Agency (RTDA)
SCOPE OF WORK
01

Marine & Jetty Works

Delivery of the offshore jetty and associated in-water structural works forming the marine interface of the port.

02

Ground & Foundation Works

Ground improvement, piling activities and foundation verification, including PVD installation and Pile Integrity Testing (PIT).

03

Cargo Facilities

Development of dedicated cargo-storage infrastructure to support handling and port operations.

04

Passenger & Marine Facilities

Development of the passenger terminal together with the police pontoon and floating-boat facilities.

05

Utilities & Power

Integration of essential port utilities and power-substation infrastructure serving the development.

06

Road Infrastructure

Development of the road network connecting and supporting movement throughout the port facilities.
