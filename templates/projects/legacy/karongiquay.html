Karongi Auxiliary Quay

CEC delivered the Karongi Auxiliary Quay through the design, procurement and construction of marine foundation and quay works.

The project included pile foundations in weathered rock, deck reinforcement and concrete casting, marine equipment installation, together with land reclamation and dredging activities.

LOCATION
Karongi, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
Marine & Civil Infrastructure
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

PILE FOUNDATION WORKS

Construction of 24 pile foundations, each approximately 1 metre in diameter, founded in weathered rock and provided with permanent steel lining.

02

PILE CAGE & DRILLING WORKS

Execution of pile cage lowering and drilling operations required for the marine foundation system.

03

PILE INTEGRITY TESTING

Execution of Pile Integrity Tests (PIT) to verify the quality and integrity of the completed pile foundations.

04

DECK REINFORCEMENT & CONCRETE

Installation of reinforcement for the quay deck together with concrete casting works.

05

MARINE EQUIPMENT

Installation of marine equipment including fenders and bollards required for quay operations.

06

LAND RECLAMATION & DREDGING

Execution of land reclamation and dredging activities as part of the overall quay development.
