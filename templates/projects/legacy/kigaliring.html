Kigali Ring 110 kV Transmission Line

Construction of a new 110 kV transmission line from the existing Jabana Substation to a new 110/30 kV substation at Gahanga, supporting reliable distribution of electrical energy in Kigali.

LOCATION
Jabana–Gahanga, Kigali
PROJECT TYPE
110 kV Transmission Line
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
ELTEL Networks Sweden

SCOPE OF WORK

01

Topographic Survey

Detailed topographic study of the transmission line route.

02

Line Route Establishment

Establishment of the route between Jabana and Gahanga.

03

Tower Foundation Pegging

Pegging of the tower foundation locations along the route.

04

Tower Foundations

Construction of foundations for the transmission towers.

05

Tower Equipment Assembly

Installation and assembly of line tower equipment.

06

Tower Erection

Erection of towers for the new 110 kV transmission line.
