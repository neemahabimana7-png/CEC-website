Luxury Apartments – Kagarama

CEC developed and constructed a residential development in Kagarama, Kicukiro, comprising eight blocks with a total of 64 modern apartments.

The project covered the full development process from concept design through the execution of the substructure and superstructure, together with the integration of key Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) systems.

LOCATION
Kagarama, Kicukiro
PROJECT TYPE
Residential Development
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

CONCEPT DESIGN

Development of the project concept and design approach for the eight-block residential development.

02

SUBSTRUCTURE WORKS

Execution of the foundations and supporting substructure for the apartment blocks.

03

SUPERSTRUCTURE WORKS

Delivery of the principal building structures forming the 64-apartment residential development.

04

MECHANICAL SYSTEMS

Integration of mechanical building services required for the functionality of the residential blocks.

05

ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS

Integration of electrical systems across the apartment development as part of the coordinated MEP works.

06

PLUMBING SYSTEMS

Delivery of plumbing systems serving the apartments and associated building facilities.
