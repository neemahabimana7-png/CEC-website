Lebanon Hotel (3 Star)

CEC delivered the three-storey Lebanon Hotel in Remera as an integrated hospitality development combining structural works, building services and architectural finishing.

The project covered the design and execution of the substructure and superstructure, together with plumbing, electrical systems, air conditioning, interior finishing and swimming-pool works.

LOCATION
Remera, Kigali
PROJECT TYPE
Hospitality Development
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

Substructure Works

Execution of the foundations and supporting substructure forming the base of the three-storey hotel.

02

Superstructure & Structural Erection

Development of the hotel superstructure together with the structural erection required for the main building.

03

Electrical Systems

Integration of electrical services throughout the hotel to support its operational requirements.

04

Plumbing & Air Conditioning

Delivery of plumbing services and air-conditioning systems as part of the coordinated building-services package.

05

Interior Finishing

Completion of the hotel's internal spaces through the required architectural and finishing works.

06

Swimming Pool

Delivery of the swimming-pool facility as part of the overall hospitality development.
