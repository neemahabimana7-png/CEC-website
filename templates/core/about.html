WHO WE ARE

Century Engineering Contractors (CEC Ltd) is a Civil Engineering and Electromechanical Contractor delivering projects across energy, construction and infrastructure.

CEC specializes in Power Transmission and Distribution Projects, Power Generation Infrastructure Projects, Real Estate, Affordable Housing and Community Development Projects, Water Supply and Sanitation Projects, and Marine and Civil Construction related Works.

OUR PURPOSE

Vision

Becoming Africa’s preferred Contractor in Civil and Electro-mechanical Engineering.

Mission

To provide high-quality construction and engineering services that are sustainable & affordable to the African Community.

Core Values

Value for Money, Quality and Delivery, Innovation, Timely and Expected Cost Delivery, Integrity, Safety & Environment Preservation.

OUR EXPERTISE

Civil & Structural Engineering

Substructure, superstructure, structural erection and associated civil works.

Electromechanical Engineering

Integrated electromechanical solutions including electrical, mechanical, plumbing and related building systems.

Power Infrastructure

Transmission lines, substations, distribution networks and associated electrical infrastructure.

Building & Infrastructure Development

Residential, commercial, institutional and community development infrastructure.

Marine & Civil Construction

Ports, quays, jetties, deep foundations and associated marine and civil works.

ENGINEERING MILESTONE

A First for Rwandan Engineering

CEC Ltd made history by becoming the first Rwandan company to successfully build a 220kV Transmission Line, constructing a portion of the Shango-Birembo Line interconnecting Rwanda and DRC.
