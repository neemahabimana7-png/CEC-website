ELECTROMECHANICAL / MEP ENGINEERING

CEC delivers integrated Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) solutions for buildings and infrastructure projects, coordinating essential building systems from installation through commissioning.

Our MEP works are designed to enhance comfort, safety, efficiency and long-term performance of every facility we build.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Mechanical Systems

Installation of mechanical systems including pumps, piping, equipment and associated components.

Plumbing Systems

Installation of plumbing systems, water supply, drainage, sanitary systems and fixtures.

Electrical Systems

Power distribution, lighting, earthing, wiring and installation of electrical equipment.

HVAC & Air Conditioning

Supply and installation of HVAC systems for ventilation, cooling and climate control.

Fire Protection Systems

Design and installation of fire detection, alarm and suppression systems.

Testing & Commissioning

Testing, inspection and commissioning of all MEP systems to ensure safety, compliance and optimal performance.
