WHO WE ARE

Century Engineering Contractors (CEC Ltd) is a Civil Engineering and Electromechanical Contractor delivering projects across energy, construction and infrastructure.

CEC specializes in Power Transmission and Distribution Projects, Power Generation Infrastructure Projects, Real Estate, Affordable Housing and Community Development Projects, Water Supply and Sanitation Projects, and Marine and Civil Construction related Works.

OUR PURPOSE

ENGINEERING MILESTONE

A First for Rwandan Engineering

CEC Ltd made history by becoming the first Rwandan company to successfully build a 220kV Transmission Line, constructing a portion of the Shango-Birembo Line interconnecting Rwanda and DRC.
