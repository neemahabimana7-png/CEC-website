220kV Shango–
Birembo Transmission Line

CEC Ltd successfully executed the construction of the 220kV transmission line from Shango to Birembo, strengthening Rwanda’s national grid and improving power reliability between Rwanda and DRC.

LOCATION
Shango–Birembo, Rwanda
PROJECT TYPE
220kV Transmission Line
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Rwanda Energy Group (REG)
SCOPE OF WORK
01

Route Survey & Tower Pegging

Identification and setting out of tower locations along the transmission route.

02

Excavation Works

Excavation and preparation of tower foundation locations.

03

Tower Foundations

Construction of foundations supporting the 220kV transmission towers.

04

Tower Erection

Assembly and erection of transmission towers along the line.

05

Conductor Stringing

Installation and stringing of transmission conductors.

06

Associated Civil Works

Supporting civil works required for transmission-line construction.
