Akagera Game Lodge

CEC carried out the refurbishment and upgrade of Akagera Game Lodge, covering both civil works and electrical supply.

The project involved detailed surveying, design, construction, and the installation of Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) systems, together with electrical supply and commissioning.

LOCATION
Akagera National Park
PROJECT TYPE
Hospitality Refurbishment
STATUS
Completed
CLIENT
Private
SCOPE OF WORK
01

SURVEYING

Detailed surveying of the existing lodge facilities to support the refurbishment and upgrade works.

02

DESIGN

Development of the design requirements for the planned civil, building-services and electrical improvements.

03

CIVIL CONSTRUCTION

Execution of the civil construction works required for the refurbishment and upgrading of the lodge facilities.

04

MEP INSTALLATION

Installation and integration of Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing systems throughout the project.

05

ELECTRICAL SUPPLY

Delivery of the electrical supply works required to support the operation of the upgraded lodge facilities.

06

COMMISSIONING

Testing and commissioning of the completed systems and installations before operational handover.
