HISTORICAL ACHIEVEMENTS

Building Milestones.
Creating Impact.

CEC Ltd made the history of being the first Rwandan company that successfully built a 220kV Transmission Line; a portion of Shango-Birembo Line interconnecting Rwanda and DRC.

CEC Ltd is also renowned for the design and construction of Rwanda's first commercial port on the scenic shores of Lake Kivu in Rubavu District.
