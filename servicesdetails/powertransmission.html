POWER TRANSMISSION & DISTRIBUTION

CEC delivers end-to-end solutions for high-voltage transmission, substation and distribution networks.

We connect communities, power industries and drive progress with safe, reliable and efficient electrical infrastructure.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Transmission Line Projects

Construction of 220kV and 110kV transmission lines connecting key regions and substations.

Substation & Transformer Works

Design, construction, installation and maintenance of substations and power transformers.

Distribution Networks

Construction of MV/LV distribution networks, feeder interconnections and rural electrification.

Testing & Commissioning

Testing, protection and commissioning of transmission lines, substations and distribution systems.

Protection & Control Systems

Installation of protection, SCADA and control systems for reliable power operations.

Operation & Maintenance Support

Routine maintenance, inspections and support services to ensure uninterrupted power supply.
