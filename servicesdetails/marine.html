MARINE & PORT CONSTRUCTION

CEC delivers specialized marine and port construction solutions for complex waterfront infrastructure. Our capabilities span quay and jetty construction, deep foundations and piling, port facilities, marine structures, land reclamation, dredging and associated civil infrastructure.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Quay & Jetty Construction

Construction of offshore and in-water quay, jetty and waterfront structures.

Deep Foundations & Piling

Bored piling, pile installation, permanent steel lining, pile integrity testing and deep foundation works.

Port Infrastructure

Construction of cargo facilities, passenger terminals, port utilities, road networks and supporting infrastructure.

Marine Structures & Equipment

Construction and installation of pontoons, floating-boat facilities, fenders, bollards and associated marine structures.

Land Reclamation & Dredging

Land reclamation,dredging and site development supporting marine infrastructure.

Civil & Structural Works

Reinforced concrete structures, deck construction, foundations and associated civil engineering works.

BUILDING RESILIENT MARINE INFRASTRUCTURE.CONNECTING COMMUNITIES & COMMERCE.

Through engineering expertise, quality construction and a commitment to safety, CEC delivers durable marine and port infrastructure designed for demanding waterfront environments.

Engineering Rwanda's future through dependable civil, electrical and electromechanical solutions built to last.
