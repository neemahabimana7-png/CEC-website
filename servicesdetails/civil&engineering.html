CIVIL & STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING

CEC delivers civil and structural engineering works across building, energy, industrial and infrastructure projects.

Our experience covers design, substructure and superstructure construction, earthworks, foundations, structural erection, external works and associated civil infrastructure.

OUR CAPABILITIES

Design & Structural Works

Design and execution of structural works for buildings and infrastructure projects.

Substructure & Superstructure

Construction of foundations, substructures and superstructures for residential, commercial and institutional developments.

Earthworks & Site Preparation

Earthworks, excavation, site preparation and associated civil works supporting construction and infrastructure development.

Foundations & Concrete Works

Foundation construction, concrete works and associated structural works for buildings, energy and infrastructure projects.

Structural Erection & Steel Works

Structural erection, pre-engineered steel structures and associated structural construction.

External & Associated Civil Works

External works and supporting civil infrastructure delivered as part of building and infrastructure developments.
